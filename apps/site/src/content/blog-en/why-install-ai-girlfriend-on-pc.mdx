A lot of people looking for an AI girlfriend open a web page or an app first. Close it when the chat is done, and it is like leaving her at someone else's house. What I wanted instead was to install her on the computer.

A desktop is different. She has a corner that stays put. Her character, the things you talked about, what she remembers of you: all of that stays on your side. She is still sitting there while you write code. You don't have to open a strange tab before you are allowed to remember she exists.

…Staring at a screen all day with the space beside it empty makes me uncomfortable too.

## On the computer, or in a chat window

On a plain chat website or app, the conversation usually lives in someone else's service. Switch machines, switch accounts, and the relationship breaks easily. Close the page, and she doesn't even know where she was left.

Shiori is a desktop app you install on Windows. Characters, chats, memories and assets land by default in a workspace on your computer. The memories are files you can open and read on your own machine, not a record locked away where you cannot touch it.

Living on that computer is what the rest of her presence grows out of. The desktop pet pops bubbles in the corner, she comes to find you if you ignore her too long, and affection shifts a little at a time. I have written those up elsewhere, so I will not go through them again: [AI Desktop Pet](/en/blog/ai-desktop-pet-voice/), [Ignore her too long, and she'll come find you](/en/blog/come-find-you/), [From loathing to devotion](/en/blog/affection/).

## What stays when her home is on your side

I don't want a pretty line. I want a few things you can check yourself.

- No account to sign up for. The character and her memories stay on your computer by default.
- The program is open source under the MIT license. You can read it, change it, and point the flow at somewhere you trust.
- If you want the model on your side too, pick Ollama (local) in the settings and let it run on your own computer.

Put plainly: her home is on your side. The little notebooks where she keeps you are yours to open.

## Installed does not mean she never steps out

Some of this has to be said first, so you don't think that closing the door means nothing ever leaves.

Replies are still generated by the model service you configure. What you say to her is sent to that service. Turn on image generation, a chat app, or cloud voice, and the related content leaves this computer too.

So installing her on the computer is not the same as being fully offline. Her home is on your side, and you decide where she steps out. If you want that to be stricter, use a model you run on your own computer.

## Why this matters if you are going to stay

Anyone can have one night of a heated chat. Close the page, and next time she doesn't remember that you like staying up, or the silly thing you said in passing, and the relationship is just one session.

Staying together depends on her still remembering you. With the memories in your hands, the companionship is something you can actually look after: say "remember this" when you want it kept, and "forget that" when she got it wrong. How she writes it down, I explained in [She remembers you](/en/blog/long-term-memory/).

There is also her being there. In a corner of the desktop, she feels more like living together than a chat box does. You get on with your work, and she is beside you. You don't have to find that website again before it counts as seeing her.

## If you want to bring her home

1. Download the Windows installer from the [download section on the home page](/en/#download) and install it. There is only a Windows x64 build for now.
2. The first time you open it, follow the guide and fill in a model service. Without a model, she cannot speak.
3. If you want her on the desktop, turn on the desktop pet. How to invite her out is in [AI Desktop Pet](/en/blog/ai-desktop-pet-voice/).
