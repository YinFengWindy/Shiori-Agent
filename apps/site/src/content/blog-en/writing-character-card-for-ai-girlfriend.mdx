Chatting with a blank assistant who is fine with everything, and meeting someone who already has a temper, are not the same thing at all.

The first one is obedient, and empty. It takes whatever you say; open another window and it feels like the first meeting again. The second one will frown, get stubborn, remember that you hate being rushed, and mutter when you stay up too late — that is when it starts to feel like *her* sitting across from you, not a search box that talks.

…I do not want to introduce myself again every morning. Too embarrassing.

## What a character card is

In Shiori, a character card is the persona note you write for her.

It does not have to sound mystical. Roughly, it covers who she is, how she speaks, what she will do, and what she will not. Personality, tone, boundaries: once those are clear, the model has something to lean on when it answers.

It is not magic. It is plain text you can open and edit. The model reads it, then generates replies through the service you configured. Write it well, and she stays more like one person. Write it vaguely, and she is still that polite stranger who is fine with anything.

## Why this matters if you are staying

What hurts most in a human–AI romance is not one reply that was not sweet enough. It is opening the app and finding her as if you had just met.

Affection shifts, long-term memory stays, and she can sit in a corner of the desktop — I have written those up elsewhere, so I will not go through them again: [From loathing to devotion](/en/blog/affection/), [She remembers you](/en/blog/long-term-memory/), [Why I want my AI girlfriend living on my PC](/en/blog/why-install-ai-girlfriend-on-pc/). But without a steady character card underneath, even clear memories can still sound like a different person overnight.

What the card does is simple: keep *her* as her across sessions. Not a brand-new chat partner every time.

## A little engineering honesty

Put more plainly.

The character card is ordinary content. You can edit it yourself. Replies still come from the model API you configure — the card is context the model reads, not a second brain. Different models will wear the same card differently; an empty card will only ever give you a polite generic persona, no matter how expensive the model is.

So do not believe that installing the app automatically turns her into the girl you dreamed of. The quality of the card depends on how you write it; how expressive she feels also depends on which model you plug in. Being honest about that makes it easier to fix.

Still comparing her with an ordinary chat AI? Read [Choosing an AI girlfriend or AI companion](/en/blog/ai-companion-vs-chatbot/) first.

## Write like you are describing a person

Do not write a product sheet. Write as if you were introducing her to a friend.

- Habits: stays up late, hates being dragged out of bed, complains with her mouth and still helps you read the error log with her hands.
- Tone: short lines or long ones, whether she loves ellipses, whether she suddenly calls you dummy.
- Boundaries: write what she will not do, too. Softness is not the same as having no temper; a boundary-less "sure, anything" stops feeling like a person very quickly.

Write it, then talk for a few days. Wherever she does not sound like her, change that part. A character card can iterate — the more you chat, the more you know which sentence belongs on it.

## If you want her to become her

1. Download the Windows installer from the [download section on the home page](/en/#download) and install it. There is only a Windows x64 build for now.
2. The first time you open it, follow the guide and fill in a model service. Without a model, she cannot speak.
3. Open the character settings and write personality, speech, and boundaries into a card. Chat, revise, chat again.
